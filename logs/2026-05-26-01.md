## Key Takeaways

- A limit looks at what a function *approaches* as x gets close to a value — not what it equals there. This is why we can cancel (x − a) factors: we're never actually plugging in x = a.
- For piecewise limits: identify which piece applies just to the left and just to the right of the target, then evaluate each at the target. If they match, the limit exists.
- End behavior rule: divide numerator and denominator by the highest power in the denominator. Same degree → ratio of leading coefficients. Top lower → 0. Top higher → ∞.
