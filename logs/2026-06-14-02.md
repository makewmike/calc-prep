## Key Takeaways

- Difference of squares: x² − a² = (x + a)(x − a). Recognize it on sight.
- Negative exponents flip to the denominator: x^(−n) = 1/xⁿ.
- Radicals are fractional exponents: ∛(x²) = x^(2/3). You'll rewrite these constantly in Calc.
- Factor-and-cancel on rational expressions is the same move as resolving 0/0 limits — the connection is real.
