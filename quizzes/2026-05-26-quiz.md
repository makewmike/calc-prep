# Quiz — Limits Intuition & Behavior
**Date:** 2026-05-26 | **Session:** 1

---

## Problems & Answers

**P1.** Evaluate: \( \lim_{x \to 3} (2x + 1) \)

> **Answer:** 7. Direct substitution — function is continuous, no issues at x = 3.

---

**P2.** Evaluate: \( \lim_{x \to 5} \dfrac{x^2 - 25}{x - 5} \)

> **Answer:** 10. Factor numerator as (x−5)(x+5), cancel (x−5), evaluate x+5 at x=5.

---

**P3.** Given:
\[
f(x) = \begin{cases} x + 1 & x < 2 \\ 5 & x = 2 \\ 3x - 2 & x > 2 \end{cases}
\]
Does \( \lim_{x \to 2} f(x) \) exist?

> **Answer:** DNE. Left-hand limit: x+1 → 3. Right-hand limit: 3x−2 → 4. They don't match. f(2)=5 is irrelevant to the limit.

---

**P3 Follow-up.** Given:
\[
g(x) = \begin{cases} 2x & x < 1 \\ x + 1 & x \geq 1 \end{cases}
\]
Does \( \lim_{x \to 1} g(x) \) exist?

> **Answer:** Yes, limit = 2. Left: 2x → 2. Right: x+1 → 2. Both sides agree.

---

**P4.** Evaluate: \( \lim_{x \to \infty} \dfrac{3x^2 + 5}{x^2 - 1} \)

> **Answer:** 3. Same degree — ratio of leading coefficients = 3/1 = 3. Divide by x²: (3 + 5/x²)/(1 − 1/x²) → 3.

---

**P4 Follow-up.** Evaluate: \( \lim_{x \to \infty} \dfrac{7x^2 - x}{2x^2 + 3} \)

> **Answer:** 7/2. Same degree — ratio of leading coefficients.

---

**P5.** Evaluate: \( \lim_{x \to 2} \dfrac{1}{x - 2} \)

> **Answer:** DNE. From right → +∞. From left → −∞. Vertical asymptote at x=2.

---

**P6.** Evaluate: \( \lim_{x \to 4} \dfrac{x^2 - 16}{x - 4} \)

> **Answer:** 8. Factor: (x+4)(x−4)/(x−4) = x+4. Hole at x=4, no asymptote. Limit = 8.

---

**P7.** Evaluate: \( \lim_{x \to \infty} \dfrac{4x + 7}{x^2 - 3} \)

> **Answer:** 0. Top degree (1) < bottom degree (2). Divide by x²: (4/x + 7/x²)/(1 − 3/x²) → 0.
